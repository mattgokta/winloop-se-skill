Call yesterday, July 29, with the IT Director (the customer has named him the technical decision owner for this evaluation), their Identity Architect, and the Application Owner for the legacy claims-processing app. Background for whoever reads this later: that app is ancient and homegrown, with an undocumented session layer — some in-house token cache nobody on their side fully understands and that we cannot reproduce in our labs no matter what we stand up. The one thing still open on their side is whether we can actually terminate active sessions in that app fast enough once their risk signal fires, under their real network conditions (a proxy chain plus a segmented test network that throttles east-west traffic). IT Director was blunt: this is a mandatory selection criterion, and they will not select any vendor that cannot prove it in their environment.

We have genuinely tried everything short of a POC. Sent the session-management docs back in May — Identity Architect read them and said they tell him nothing about their app. Ran a focused live demo of session termination in June. Did an architecture session with their network team after that. Two weeks ago we ran the guided workshop — the labs passed, but everyone in the room agreed the labs cannot represent the homegrown token cache. Still unresolved after all of it.

So on this call we agreed a POC. One proposition only: when the defined risk signal fires, terminate at least 99% of test sessions in the legacy claims app within 60 seconds. Scope: that single application, synthetic accounts only, their test environment, the dataset the Application Owner already prepared (about 200 synthetic users). Owners: Application Owner stands up the environment and accounts by Friday August 1; Identity Architect runs the test harness and owns pass/fail measurement; I own the integration and policy configuration on our side. Ten business days, August 3 through August 14. Technical decision meeting is already on their calendar for August 18, and the IT Director said that if the POC hits the number, that closes the last open criterion and he will give us the technical decision at that meeting.

One thought from my side: while we're in there we could also show them the full platform — governance, PAM, device posture — since we'll have their test environment anyway. Feels like a chance to expand the footprint.
